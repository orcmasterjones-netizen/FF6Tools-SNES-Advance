# FF6Tools for SNES Advance v0.7

This offline editor lets you build and edit content for **SNES Advance Regular v1.6.5**. Open Narshe in the map editor, click a resident, and follow their event directly to the line they speak. You can paint a room, place a chest, choose its reward, and connect the room to the Dragon’s Den from that same view.

- Paint field and world maps, place NPCs, edit triggers and follow exits to their destinations. The map preview uses the game's sprite pose table.
- Edit monster AI, formations, items, spells and Espers. Import whole images or selected regions with SNES palette generation.
- Open a SNES Advance Regular ROM, or import a clean or supported modified Final Fantasy III USA v1.0 or v1.1 ROM. Export saves a separate file.

**Getting started.** Extract the entire ZIP, open `index.html` in your browser, and choose **Open ROM**. Supply your own game ROM. All editing runs locally.

**Tested on Windows.** Chrome 157 and Firefox 160 each passed all 16 offline checks, including export, reload and file comparison. See `VALIDATION.md` for the reports and in-game checks.
